How qualifying works now

2026 cycle onward
01

Winners go first

Each age-group winner, men and women separately, gets an automatic slot. If they decline, it rolls to second, then third.

02

Everyone else is age-graded

Every remaining slot goes into one Performance Pool. Each athlete's finish time is multiplied by their group's Kona Standard, and the whole field is ranked on that number regardless of age or sex.

03

The list rolls down

Slots go to the highest-ranked athletes who accept, in order. Roughly 3,000 Kona slots exist across the season; at a typical race, two to four percent of finishers get one.
